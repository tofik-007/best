EcoGlow Lamp: a solar-powered
sustainable lighting system

A working model showing how sunlight becomes electricity — and how that electricity lights a room. Built with a solar panel, a resistor, a switch, and one LED.

01 Abstract

The EcoGlow Lamp is a simple working model that shows how sunlight can be changed into electricity to power a small LED light. A solar panel collects sunlight and converts it into electrical energy. This energy is used to glow an LED through a simple circuit.

Objective 01

Show how solar energy can be used in real life.

Objective 02

Understand how electricity flows in a simple circuit.

Objective 03

Build a low-cost, eco-friendly lamp.

Expected Impact

This project helps us understand clean energy and encourages the use of renewable energy instead of polluting energy sources.

02 Introduction

We use electricity every day, but most of it comes from non-renewable sources like coal and oil, which harm the environment.

Problem Statement

If these energy sources run out in the future, we will need alternative energy systems that are clean and renewable.

Significance of Study

This project helps students understand renewable energy and shows how small devices can be powered using natural sources like sunlight.

  • To build a working solar-powered lamp
  • To understand energy conversion (light → electricity → light)
  • To explore eco-friendly technology

03 Methodology

This project is a simple experimental model. A real working prototype (EcoGlow Lamp) is built to show how solar energy works in a circuit.

We Observe

  • Does the LED light up when exposed to light?
  • Does the switch control the circuit?
  • How strong is the light depending on sunlight?

Materials Used

  • Small solar panel
  • White LED
  • 220Ω resistor
  • ON/OFF switch
  • Wires / jumper wires
  • Cardboard or small box (lamp body)

04 Steps to Make It

Fix the solar panel on top of the lamp.

Connect solar panel positive wire to switch.

Connect switch to resistor.

Connect resistor to LED positive leg.

Connect LED negative leg back to solar panel negative.

Check all connections are tight.

Shine light on the solar panel.

Turn on the switch.

The LED lights up.

Sunlight→ Solar Panel→ Electricity→ Switch→ LED

05 Discussion

The EcoGlow Lamp works successfully when enough light is available. The solar panel produces electricity which powers the LED.

Key Findings

  • Solar panels can generate electricity from light
  • Simple circuits can control energy flow
  • Renewable energy is usable in real devices

Limitations

  • Works only in light or sunlight
  • Cannot store energy without a battery

Future Research Ideas

  • Add battery for energy storage
  • Add motion or heat energy harvesting
  • Use biodegradable materials for the lamp body
  • Create multi-energy source systems
